EST. BY QUEEN MARLOWE · HER ROYAL COURT

Dear reader,

Under my command, I have allowed the Librarian to place this note into your hands.

The Royal Library is the complete and original collection of nightmares dwelling beneath the swollen floorboards, feeding upon the vitality of human beings.

It is of utmost importance that you exercise caution when crossing into this place. These monsters have a catalogue for a reason.

They will hunt you, bite you, and, once satisfied, swallow you whole — unless, of course, they decide to keep you because they find you far too interesting.

Should that be the case, you will no longer be under the Queen's protection.

With the Queen's permission, The Librarian

Some tales are romantic. Some are tragic. Others are best left undisturbed. You have been warned, darling.
