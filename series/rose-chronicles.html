The Secrets

Prologue

Before the lies, there was a small town nestled in the south of Scotland, known as Ashbourne. A town that, no matter how hard you searched the maps, you would never find. You would probably assume it had been invented by someone who had never set foot there.

But Ashbourne existed. And it still does. Following a territorial dispute, the town was divided in two. Two pieces of a puzzle whose story is far from over.

"Some things are never truly buried. They simply learn to wear another face."

History & Background

Before the division, Ashbourne was just another town in the south of Scotland, whose economy depended on a local industry controlled by influential families. For generations, these families had accumulated land, property, and political power. The town grew around this structure, and a great number of its inhabitants depended, directly or indirectly, on that industry to survive.

Until the company fell into crisis and shut its doors.

Hundreds of people lost their jobs. Small businesses disappeared, families lost their homes, and the inequality that had once been concealed beneath an illusion of stability became impossible to ignore.

That was when the dispute began.

The traditional families wanted to preserve their properties and influence. Others advocated for land redistribution, new investments, and a different administration. The disagreement escalated into a territorial and political dispute. After months of conflict, Ashbourne was officially divided into two districts.

But the division solved nothing.

The Rose Chronicles was the first district to be established. At the forefront stood the Ashford and Kensington families, promising to remain devoted to tradition and refusing to surrender everything they had built simply to satisfy people who thought more with their stomachs than with their minds.
