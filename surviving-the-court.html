01 / A WORD OF WARNING

Welcome to the Court

Your Queen has a rather strong inclination towards embracing the darker and more creative corners of her mind. It matters little whether an idea turns out beautiful or utterly grotesque, or whether it becomes so unsettling that the reader feels compelled to look away. Whatever it may be, it will be entirely hers.

And by hers, I mean mine alone.

By choosing Queen's Court as your new travel destination, you will find yourself facing an entirely unfamiliar territory. You will encounter lies, lust, arrogance, manipulation, and two districts competing against one another. We do not know how far Queen's Court will expand... We do not know whether, someday, other cities like Ashbourne will decide it is time to scatter into several smaller pieces, much like the fragments of a puzzle. If that happens, the castle may very well have to become an orphanage for bloodlines damaged by the choices of their parents, or someone else's parents.

However, until any of that comes to pass, put on your shoes and let us take a stroll through the streets of Rose Chronicles and Moonlit Chronicles. I shall prepare you for the dangers you may encounter out there.

Her Majesty has decided what deserves to be revealed.

02 / THE UNWRITTEN RULES

Rules of the Kingdom

Some advice for those entering the Queen's domain.

I mentioned embracing the darker corners of one's mind, so I suppose I should make it clear that every story has a reason behind it. Come now, did you truly believe you would read about the districts and find that there was nothing more to them than what I had already revealed? Of course not. Your Queen possesses far more imagination than you could possibly fathom.

It is up to you to decide what you wish to discover and what you are willing to face, but I have one question: are you prepared to deal with the consequences?

EVERY SINGLE character in this Court — yes, without exception — has their own problems, whether displayed for all to see or locked away behind seven keys. Keep in mind that I will not expose each and every one of them as though I were keeping a diary about laboratory rats. It shall be your responsibility to discover what is wrong with them, and what is not — should you dare to get to know them, of course.

It is worth mentioning that I will not tolerate character theft. Goodness, these grotesque little creatures have hearts, just like you do. However, inspired characters are more than welcome in the Royal Court! Provided they are of legal age and do not go around kissing their own siblings — we accept neither minors nor characters with romantic interests in those who came from the same womb or share their blood.

Furthermore, all POVs are female. No exceptions. Your Queen shall make use of them as well, whether for research purposes or simply for the amusement of tending to something I have created. Therefore, there is a considerable chance that a member of one of the families may suddenly disappear because Her Majesty is refining their inner workings to ensure they react as they should. That is, of course, if there are any flaws in need of correction.

I shall also tolerate no form of discrimination within these walls! Be it based on skin colour, religion, musical taste, nationality, sexuality, or even whether one person stands to relieve themselves while another prefers to sit down.

So mind your tongues, visitors. For it may not be the characters who disappear from the Royal Court, but you. And I assure you, not even your dust will be found.

03 / KNOW YOUR SURROUNDINGS

Who resides within the Court?

Before you get to know the inhabitants of the districts — now that you have already wandered through their filthy streets — I must warn you about what you might encounter and how you might encounter it. Perhaps I shall list these things, perhaps I shall not. Nevertheless, they will be here for you to read and understand that some characters may cause discomfort due to the grotesque themes they involve.

You may come across religious themes, psychological and physical manipulation, and uncomfortable subjects such as sexual coercion, non-con, graphic violence, gore, body horror, torture (whether psychological or otherwise), mutilation, cannibalism, and the forced disappearance of people...

Furthermore, some characters may be absolute fucking assholes who exercise coercive control over others, engage in gaslighting, emotionally manipulate them, force pregnancies or marriages, and commit domestic violence...

Other things worth keeping an eye on include dub-con, somnophilia, degradation, mind control, moral corruption, religious fanaticism, misogyny, and social exclusion and ostracism (much like the Ashcroft family — because apparently, being wealthy and influential is enough for people to forget whose wife you fucked when she wasn't yours).

But sometimes, many of the things mentioned above may not even appear. Still, I shall leave you with my ever-famous warning: Dead Dove.

Protect yourselves! Just because Her Majesty has created a morally questionable bot does not mean her entire audience should use it.

One more important warning, since some people seem incapable of distinguishing fiction from reality: I do not condone anything I write. I do it for my own amusement, but it is worth making clear that I have an aversion to all of these things outside the realm of pseudonyms, fictional identities, and equally fictional worlds.
