01 / THE WOMAN BEHIND THE CROWN

A name behind the crown, a mind behind the stories.

♛

Among Gothic books and fantasies, there is a woman who has only just stepped into adulthood. She lives an ordinary life, much like everyone else, yet her mind is undoubtedly tormented in every way imaginable. But should we really call creativity a torment? Or should we call it a love for art itself?

Born in southeastern Brazil, she comes from a family in which luxury was never the center of attention. Instead, it was hard work — the kind her parents endured simply so she could have a good life. And by good, I mean one in which there would always be food on the table. Since childhood, her imagination has been as fertile as the soil in which fruits and vegetables were grown. She was always good at inventing stories, but even better at living them. After all, no one can put a stop to a creative child with an imagination as vast as hers.

Books were her first love, and writing quietly became her second, without ever having to take its place. To her, every form of art has always had room in her heart; there was no need to dig deeper to make more space. However, we are women cursed from the very womb with an insatiable thirst for knowledge, with the longing to have our work known by others. Cursed to carry art as our coat of arms in a world that condemns it.

Marlowe — or Marlowye, it really makes no difference — is the pseudonym of a twenty-year-old Brazilian woman who carries with her the dream of becoming someone whose name is known, not necessarily for the excellence of her work, but for the wonderful idea of having her worlds and characters read by others. And so, amidst an ordinary, uneventful life — which, I should mention, comes with plenty of bills to pay — she decided to bring the Queen's Court project to life. It is not a grand project, but it is hers. I mean, mine.

I worked on this website for five days straight, often staying up late into the night, and as I watched it slowly take shape, I realized that conquering the world does not seem so difficult, as long as you are willing to try — as long as you have dreams.

I am not perfect. I certainly have my flaws, and I make mistakes, but I have something I can call my own. And I hope, with all my heart and soul, that you will enjoy your time here. That you will give these characters a chance — characters born from a glimmer of hope that emerged amidst the turmoil of an unstable mind. And I hope you understand that fear can be dreadful and terrifying, but it can also be the very thing that pushes you forward.

Make your fear a step towards victory.

Marlowe

02 / THE QUEEN'S INTERESTS

Things that haunt her thoughts and capture her heart.

There are things that simply cannot be hidden, such as a passion that makes your heart sink into your chest and butterflies dance wildly in your stomach. However, don't get me wrong: my passions are books and writing, as I mentioned before. I am fascinated by every world, language, and plot I create so effortlessly, and I hold an enormous affection for my characters — although I usually do nothing but make them suffer, so I suppose I feel a little sorry for them sometimes.

However, beyond pages and pens, horror and melancholic films hold my heart in their hands as though it were a precious jewel. I have a taste for the adrenaline of jump scares and psychological horror, as they make me feel truly alive. Melancholic films, on the other hand, always seem to carry a life lesson alongside a sense of solitude. I also have a fondness for fantasy, whether it comes in the form of films, books, or music.

Speaking of music, it has always accompanied my daily daydreams and my quiet, pathetic little life. I am almost always listening to something, and my taste seems to wander from folk to rock, from gothic music to Brazilian MPB. That is simply how I am made, and if nothing changes, I suppose that is how I will remain.

However, when I venture into the darker corners of my mind, I find an overwhelming longing for freedom. I want to show everyone who I am and what I am like. After all, when I took a closer look at every corner of myself, I realized that I am more broken than a porcelain doll after crashing onto a cold floor. I am fascinated by life, but also by death; I long for romance while simultaneously wishing for no man to ever lay a hand on me; I want people to read my books, yet anxiety takes the lead and condemns them before the audience even gets the chance. I admire the occult, everything society despises and fears, and with this feeling firmly lodged in my chest, I study and seek to understand why the frightening fascinates me so much.

I have come to realize that I have my peculiarities, that I am flawed, and that I may seem frightening for enjoying subjects most people would rather avoid. Yet many of those same people possess a far darker side, one capable of crossing boundaries that I, as imperfect as I am, would never dare to cross.

So, if you wish to know more about my tastes, passions, longings, and dreams... my Discord is somewhere around here, playing a little game of hide-and-seek.

03 / THE CREATIVE MIND

Between ink and imagination.

I have always loved inventing worlds, stories, and entire scripts detailing what my characters would do and who they would become. And if you are here, it is because you took an interest in the little town I created and the two districts that eventually became part of it. However, I always thought this sort of thing would only work in fantasy. But this is no fantasy.

Because, let's be honest, the story of Ashbourne and its troublesome families is hardly filled with dragons and elves — unless, of course, we are talking about the purest form of horror: corruption and lies.

And I must confess that all of this is also part of what I write. We tend to think that everything in books will be perfect, unlike in real life. But just as films and games do, books have their own cruel and unsettling sides. Writing, consequently, does too. I have created wonderful characters, characters with dreams and an insatiable hunger to conquer the world, but I have also created cruel, misogynistic characters who would kill you the very moment they laid eyes on you.

Because life imitates art, and art imitates life.

Here at Queen's Court, we have a story, a town, and dozens of families who have lost their way along the path paved by human arrogance and pride; families who have lost themselves and the very people they once loved so dearly. And so, the characters I have created — some of whom may have had nothing to do with the corruption of the wealthy — also carry stories worthy of pity. They have their own troubles, dreams, disappointments, terrors, and unstable minds shaped by years of arguments, lies, illusions, coercion, and countless other hardships.

They are not here to build a fairy tale, my dears. Fairy tales do not exist, no matter how hard one might try to create them. After all, what is a story without drama, disappointment, and loss?

What is a story without the grotesque experience of facing oneself?

04 / BEYOND THE COURT

There is more than meets the eye.

Leaving behind the uncanny valley and all those morally questionable characters, I would like to share a few silly facts about myself — perhaps this will help you understand that everything here is just a character, in case you haven't figured that out yet.

01

I love studying languages, but I tend to forget them rather quickly if I don't practice.

02

I've always loved animals, but it wasn't until adulthood that I finally stopped being terrified of snakes, rats, earthworms, and slugs. In fact, I would absolutely love to own a hognose snake and a pet rat.

03

I am probably the world's biggest insect hater, even though I try not to be (I have a phobia).

04

Speaking of phobias, I have thalassophobia. Just thinking about the open ocean is enough to make me feel frightened and uneasy.

05

I am twenty years old and, ironically enough, an Aries.

06

I was born in Brazil and have never left the country, but I intend to travel abroad once I have the money.

07

My biggest dream, besides becoming a writer, is to be independent.

08

I've considered adopting a child in the future because of my fear of marriage, even though my dream is to carry and give birth to a baby of my own when the time is right.

09

I really enjoy video games.

10

I am the kind of person who desperately craves attention and friendships, only to distance herself from everyone at the first opportunity.

11

I am demisexual.

12

I love children's cartoons, especially Hello Jadoo and Oddbods. I am also the biggest fan of The Amazing World of Gumball.

13

I learned to speak English by playing Roblox.

14

I have a gaming channel where I upload videos once every seven months.

15

I LOVE moths.

These are some rather random, utterly unrelated things, but that is precisely the point: behind a screen, pseudonyms, creativity, and imagination, I am just an ordinary woman.
